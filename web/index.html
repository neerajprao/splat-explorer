How to record a good video
  • Walk slowly and keep moving through the space; don't just turn on the spot.
  • Go around the room facing inward, then again facing the walls, at two heights.
  • Keep lighting constant: all lights on, blinds closed against direct sun.
  • Nothing moving: no people, pets, TVs or fans in view.
  • Avoid filming mirrors and glass head-on; include furniture and corners in plain walls.
  • 1–2 min for a bedroom or office, 2–4 min for a larger room. End where you started.
  • Anything the camera never saw (under the bed, behind the sofa) cannot appear in 3D.
